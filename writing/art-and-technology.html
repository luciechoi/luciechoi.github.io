The Intersection Between Art and Technology

August 2026 • Art & Tech • Lucie Choi

Technology and art are often viewed as opposite ends of the human experience — logic versus intuition, structure versus emotion. But throughout history, every major leap in artistic expression has been sparked by tools and technologies.

"When code is treated as an expressive medium, the browser transforms from a document reader into a canvas for living, dynamic visual experiences."

Code as a Creative Medium

Unlike static paint or sculpted marble, code is temporal and responsive. It can react to human presence, listen to audio input, simulate gravity, or evolve through mathematical randomness. This creates a feedback loop where the artist doesn't merely produce a finished object, but designs the rules of a living system.

The Beauty of Restraint

In an era where digital noise and complexity are ubiquitous, the most impactful interactive artworks are those that exercise aesthetic restraint. By prioritizing generous whitespace, deliberate typography, and subtle spatial dimensions, technology can evoke tranquility and curiosity.
