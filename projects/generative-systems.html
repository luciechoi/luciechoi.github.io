Generative flow field simulation generated procedurally through multi-octave Perlin noise.

Overview

A series of computational art experiments exploring how simple mathematical rules can produce complex, organic emergent visual patterns. By combining vector field calculations with autonomous agent behaviors, these simulations generate unique artistic compositions in real-time.

Key Algorithms & Techniques

  • Flow Fields & Vector Fields: Continuous 2D/3D gradient noise driving thousands of tracer particles.
  • Autonomous Steering Behaviors: Craig Reynolds' flocking and boids simulation models.
  • Cellular Automata & Reaction-Diffusion: Simulating natural pattern morphogenesis found in biological systems.
