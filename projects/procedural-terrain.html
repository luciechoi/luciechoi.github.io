Procedural mountain landscape with physical sediment transport and riverbed carving.

Overview

A procedural world generation pipeline built in Houdini and VEX that simulates geological processes over time. By modeling particle-based droplet erosion, sediment carrying capacity, and talus angle deposition, terrain surfaces exhibit natural drainage channels and realistic cliff formations.

Key Features

  • Hydraulic Droplet Simulation: Droplet-based erosion and sediment deposition algorithms executing over iterative heightfields.
  • Multi-Scale Noise Synthesis: Domain-warped fractional Brownian motion for diverse biome profiles.
  • Automated Mask Generation: Slope, curvature, and sediment flow masks driving runtime texture splatting.
