Real-time particle fluid solver simulating surface tension, boundary collisions, and splashing dynamics.

Overview

A high-performance particle-based fluid solver using Smoothed Particle Hydrodynamics (SPH). The system evaluates density, pressure, and viscosity forces across tens of thousands of particles in real time using spatial hashing on GPU compute shaders.

Key Features

  • Spatial Grid Acceleration: Compact uniform grid spatial hashing for fast neighbor particle lookups.
  • Müller & WCSPH Formulations: Weakly Compressible SPH for incompressibility and realistic wave propagation.
  • Screen-Space Fluid Rendering: Bilateral depth smoothing and normal extraction for fluid surface rendering.
