Motivation

Over the years, I felt a powerful urge to express myself. I felt that words often evaded what I truly wanted to convey. So I started learning computer graphics through Three.js, and this is my first visual piece.

The texts in the scene are some words that have influenced me significantly in 2024. These quotes by famous people have lingered in my mind, floating in my head and continuously unlocking new perspectives.

Fun Facts

  • The 3D scan of myself was created using Creality's "Otter" 3D Scanner, which uses infrared light and 4 different cameras to capture depth.
  • Hair is difficult to scan because it is thin and scatters light. I applied some lotion on my hair to make it "matte".
  • I scanned it myself, so the back of my head is missing. It took several takes.
  • Then, I became curious how my cat would look like. (It's hard for a moving object...)
  • She didn't seem happy being scanned. ¯\_(ツ)_/¯
