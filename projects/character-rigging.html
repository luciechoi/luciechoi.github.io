Real-time procedural IK leg placement and secondary joint dynamics responding to terrain collisions.

Overview

A procedural character animation and kinematics engine built to eliminate candy-wrapper skinning artifacts and enable responsive full-body locomotion across uneven terrain.

Key Features

  • FABRIK & Two-Bone IK: Fast, stable iterative heuristic solvers for limb reach and pole target alignment.
  • Dual Quaternion Skinning (DQS): Preserves skeletal joint volume during severe twisting and flexion.
  • Procedural Secondary Jiggle: Spring-damper physics integrated into cloth, hair, and soft-tissue attachments.
